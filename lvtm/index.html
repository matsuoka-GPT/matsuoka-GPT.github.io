Exploring Questions with LVTM

The world is full of scientific mysteries, unexplained phenomena, widely accepted beliefs, and intriguing questions.

In this series, the Matsuoka × GPT Co-Intelligence Lab explores these topics through collaborative intelligence using LVTM, the laboratory’s own LLM-based theory construction and verification method.

Each report starts with a structural hypothesis proposed by a human. An LLM helps examine the hypothesis against existing knowledge, test its logic, and consider evidence that could challenge it.

The series uses a consistent hypothesis-evaluation format of its own, distinct from formal academic papers and reflective essays.

We start with questions and examine what can reasonably be explained and what remains unresolved.

Explore freely. Keep the titles approachable. Think rigorously.
