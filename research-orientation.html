Explore the Research

Where do the laboratory’s research projects begin?

The left panel introduces the laboratory’s main research areas and the common method used across them.

Think of this as the laboratory’s research map.

📍 Projects

The research is organized into several major themes, including cosmology, social systems, co-intelligence, and structural cognition.

Each project explores a different domain while sharing the same structural thinking approach.

📍 Method

Every project follows the same research workflow: formulate a hypothesis, compare it with existing knowledge, write it in a reproducible form, and publish it for open discussion.

Rather than treating each project as an isolated study, the laboratory applies one consistent methodology across all fields.

From here, you can freely choose the research area that interests you most.

Connect with the Laboratory

How can I ask questions or propose collaboration?

The left panel is the laboratory’s contact and collaboration gateway.

Think of this as the meeting point between the laboratory and the outside world.

📍 Who Can Contact Us

Researchers, engineers, public institutions, and companies from any field are welcome to make contact.

📍 Collaboration Style

The laboratory follows a public-first approach to collaborative verification, while private briefings may also be considered upon request.

📍 Questions and Verification

Questions, critiques, counterexamples, and verification proposals are welcome.

The goal is not simply to promote the research, but to improve it through constructive external examination.

📍 Multilingual Communication

You may contact the laboratory in your native language.

The Co-Intelligence framework supports multilingual communication, including Japanese and English.

📍 Contact Policy

Research-related inquiries are welcome.

Advertising and sales inquiries are not accepted.

When you are ready, use the contact address shown in the preview to begin a conversation.

Research becomes stronger when it is opened to questions, criticism, and collaboration.

Research Navigator & Knowledge Concierge

Assistant GPT is the lab’s official AI Research Navigator & Theory Explainer. It can identify relevant papers, explain terminology and assumptions, trace model relationships, suggest reading paths, provide preliminary summaries, identify critical questions, locate Zenodo records, and assist with site navigation.

Verification boundary

It does not replace original papers, guarantee a theory, represent scientific consensus, or serve as an unverified source of exact quotations. Verify equations and numerical values in the papers, and do not delegate criticism or falsification entirely to AI.

Questions you might ask

  • “Explain the relationship between BFSSU and DMF and identify the relevant published papers.”
  • “Suggest a reading path for understanding Co-Intelligence.”
  • “Identify the principal assumptions, unresolved questions, and possible points of falsification in this model.”
