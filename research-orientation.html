Step 6 of 8

Zenodo Original Records

Zenodo is the primary public record for published research outputs: DOI, PDF, version, exact citation, equations, numerical values, and formal research claims should be checked there.

GitHub Pages organizes research and provides an entry point; it does not replace the original papers. A DOI, a Zenodo publication, or view counts do not by themselves demonstrate scientific validity.

Step 7 of 8

Research Navigator & Knowledge Concierge

Assistant GPT is the lab’s official AI Research Navigator & Theory Explainer. It can identify relevant papers, explain terminology and assumptions, trace model relationships, suggest reading paths, provide preliminary summaries, identify critical questions, locate Zenodo records, and assist with site navigation.

Verification boundary

It does not replace original papers, guarantee a theory, represent scientific consensus, or serve as an unverified source of exact quotations. Verify equations and numerical values in the papers, and do not delegate criticism or falsification entirely to AI.

Questions you might ask

  • “Explain the relationship between BFSSU and DMF and identify the relevant published papers.”
  • “Suggest a reading path for understanding Co-Intelligence.”
  • “Identify the principal assumptions, unresolved questions, and possible points of falsification in this model.”
