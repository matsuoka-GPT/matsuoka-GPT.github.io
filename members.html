One Concept Architect × Four Specialized AI Members

Matsuoka × GPT Thought Experiment Lab is an experimental research unit where human intuition and multiple AI systems collaborate in conceptual design, theory structuring, verification, review, and technical support.

We build co-intelligence by dividing roles, not by mixing capabilities.

About the Lab

The lab explores the nature of intelligence by combining Matsuoka’s structural intuition with the logical, analytical, and technical capabilities of AI systems. Our research spans BFSSU/DMF cosmology, co-intelligence, and structural cognitive science, with outputs published on Zenodo and GitHub Pages.
